# Terra: 7-slide draft

Hard limit: 7 slides. Take screenshots from the live site, not from a local copy, so real NASA data shows.

## 1. A farmer near Casa Grande is running out of water
- Alfalfa uses about 6 acre-feet of water per acre a year (University of Arizona).
- Central Arizona farms hold junior rights to Colorado River water, so they are cut first.
- The question: what should this field grow next so it keeps paying, keeps its soil, and fits the water that's left?
- Visual: the GRACE groundwater layer over Arizona from the app.

## 2. Terra in one sentence
- "Terra compares crop rotations for your field using 20 years of NASA data, so you can see water, soil and risk before you plant."
- Visual: full screenshot of the app with the field selected.

## 3. The NASA data, and what each one tells the farmer
- NASA POWER: 20 years of daily heat, freezes and rain, which become daily crop water demand.
- MODIS NDVI: when the field is green and when it sits bare.
- SMAP: how wet the soil runs.
- GRACE and GRACE-FO: whether the region's groundwater is going down.
- One line on satellite vs ground: satellites see every field, ground probes see one spot exactly. Terra uses the satellite view and tells you where to check on the ground.

## 4. Demo: what you grow now vs. three options
- Screenshot of the baseline card next to Option 1.
- Call out: acre-feet per acre, gallons saved, the 36-month calendar with bare months, legume seasons.

## 5. Would it have worked? Year replay and Build
- Screenshot of the green, yellow and red squares for 2005 to 2024.
- Then move the "Hotter by" slider to +3.6°F and show the ranking change.
- Then open Build, swap one season to guar, and show the scores change instantly.
- Message: Terra tests every plan against real bad years, not just averages, and lets the farmer try their own ideas.

## 6. Honest by design
- Every option says why it was picked, what Terra doesn't know, and what to check with a local expert.
- Confidence labels come from rules: approximate crop values or a field outside central Arizona lower it.
- Model check: modeled crop water use lands within 30% of published Arizona measurements for every main crop.
- Terra gives options. The farmer decides.

## 7. Impact and what's next
- About 12,000 rotations compared in a second, free, in any browser.
- Already in: English and Spanish, works offline, month-by-month plan you can add to a phone calendar.
- Next: more regions and crop calendars, real crop budgets, a soil test upload.
- Links: live site, GitHub repo, data sources list.
